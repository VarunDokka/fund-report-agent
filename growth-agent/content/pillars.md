## 1. Exam-stress / student-burnout POV — proven, core pillar, **but the win is 1–2 specific posts, not the category**

3 posts = ~65% of all views this quarter (395K of 607K). **Correction from the 2026-08-16 audit:** excluding the two outlier posts (209,496 and 116,899 views), the other 5 exam-stress posts in the account's data average 164 accounts-engaged — statistically tied with an ordinary internship post (175 average, see pillar 2). The category isn't reliably 5–25x better; two specific posts are. What actually separated those two posts from the other 5 exam-stress posts in the same category: a **specific, vivid, image-driven hook** ("floating into the stars," "rewarding myself...as if my next one isn't in 1 day") instead of a generic statement of the topic ("locked in," "brain not braining") — plus same-day posting during an actual exam period (23 May). The lever is hook specificity + real-time relevance, not the topic tag alone — see [weekly-summary.md](../analytics/weekly-summary.md) Finding 2.

**Seasonality, confirmed with date math (Finding 3):** both outlier posts date to 23 May, UK exam season. Today is 2026-08-16 — no live exam period for most students. Posting generic exam-stress content this month risks measuring against May's numbers without May's actual situational relevance. Post this pillar hard around real exam periods, deadlines, and results days; don't expect May's numbers from an August post with the same topic tag but no live seasonal hook.

**Format:** Hook-first talking head, POV text overlay, "anchor + punch" text density. See [voice-guide.md](../brand/voice-guide.md).

## 2. Internship / corporate-intern humor — proven #2, but **downgraded from "currently live" to "completed arc" as of 2026-08-16**

90–300 accounts-engaged across 8+ posts. Not viral-outlier territory, but consistent — statistically about as strong per-post as an "ordinary" (non-outlier) exam-stress post (2026-08-16 audit). **Correction, same date:** the internship ended. The 2026-08-16 audit's claim that this pillar has "no seasonality gap right now" is no longer true — it was written when the internship was still ongoing. The content opportunity now is different in kind: not day-in-the-life internship content (there's no more "in-life" to film), but a wrap-up/retrospective beat — lessons learned, what surprised him, before/after framing. This is a one-time narrative moment, not a repeatable weekly pillar anymore. Apply the hook-specificity lesson from Finding 2 regardless: the account's best internship post ("bed rotting...the internship i fought and prayed for") had a vivid-image quality; a wrap-up post needs the same, not a generic "here's what I learned" list.

**Format:** Hook-first talking head, same POV-caption convention as pillar 1, for the wrap-up post(s). After that, this pillar goes dormant until a new internship/job arc starts.

## 4. Mastery / Stoic identity layer — pillar-in-progress, not yet data-validated

The Da Vinci/Marcus Aurelius material: bymiilan-style emotion/quote entries, the Richie-arc diary entries, Personal Growth confessions, PE/finance reflection pieces. This is the identity/movement layer described in [identity.md](../brand/identity.md)'s Aspirational Archetype section — it carries the same *weight* as pillars 1–2 in how seriously it's treated, but it does not yet have its own performance data. Logged as a hypothesis in [experiments.md](../coaching/experiments.md), re-ranked here once real numbers exist. Should show up every few posts, not as filler but as the throughline that makes the account read as one story instead of scattered content.

**Format:** Sit-down philosophy ("prestige" format — low volume, one consistent camera setup), or voiceover-over-stillness for bymiilan-style entries.

## 5. Personal / occasional — not strategy

Gym check-ins, dating bits, football, nostalgia, generic wellness walks, music. Consistently the weakest performers (60–150 accounts engaged) even when they cost real effort to film. Keep posting for personal reasons if Varun wants to, but don't expect these to grow the account, and don't let them dilute a page that's starting to have a clear identity.

**Caution on vegetarian-fitness content specifically:** vegetarian fitness is core to the "body" third of the freedom narrative in identity.md, but generic gym check-ins are exactly the kind of niche/personal content that underperforms in the data. The fix per [voice-guide.md](../brand/voice-guide.md)'s script content rule: don't post gym content as a check-in — post it as proof/story ("I got abs without eating chicken — here's what actually mattered") with a specific personal story attached, or fold it into the mastery pillar (#4) rather than treating it as filler.
