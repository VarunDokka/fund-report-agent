## @nicktarmo — 20yo finance-student-turned-creator, ~19M views/90 days on IG

Full reverse-engineered breakdown from 20 of his own reel transcripts lives in [nicktarmo.txt](source-research/nicktarmo.txt). Baseline template for the hook-first talking-head format.

**Formula:**
- **Hook (first 1–2 sentences, zero lead-in):** scale/FOMO framing, framework-promise ("This is my map on how to get out of a slump"), listicle, or curiosity flip.
- **Signature clarity-promise line**, right after the hook: "Once I explain this to you, it's going to become disgustingly clear [why/what]..." — promises a specific payoff instead of vague value.
- **Status/anti-neediness pattern interrupt:** "You can watch this video or don't. I don't really care." Shows up constantly — breaks the "influencer asking for attention" energy, which paradoxically holds attention.
- **Structure:** problem → naming the invisible mechanism (DMN, dopamine hole, code/media leverage) → numbered/sequential protocol → payoff/proof (his own numbers: 19M views, 45K followers/month, $100K/year).
- **CTA:** "comment 'guide'/'prompts' and I'll send it over" — converts passive viewers into commenters (algorithmically rewarded) without asking for a follow. This is the one mechanic most directly relevant to the account's own comment-rate problem.
- **Process (his own, described explicitly in transcripts):** find outlier videos (3–5x a creator's normal view count relative to follower count) → feed them to an AI content tool → templateize → apply to own topics. This is literally the process `trend-scout` should run weekly — see [experiments.md](../coaching/experiments.md).

**Persona note:** confident bordering on contrarian, second-person direct address, data-backed, clinical vocabulary (DMN, dopamine, agency, orchestrator). This register is explicitly what the account is *not* building toward — see identity.md's Aspirational Archetype section. Mine the mechanics (outlier-hunting process, comment-bait CTA, triple hook), leave the "I don't really care" bravado and the wealth-flex proof points behind.

## Alex Hormozi — "Hook, Retain, Reward" framework

Structure: strong hook in the first seconds → one clear takeaway delivered fast, no rambling → simple CTA. Massive opening text is still standard practice; minimal captions after that. Current 2026 discourse is split — some creators/critics argue the exact visual template (same font, same colors, same "woosh" sound) has become so widely copied it's lost its edge as a *pattern interrupt* specifically because it's no longer novel.
- **Read for this account:** the underlying structure (hard hook → single clear payoff → simple CTA) is sound and consistent with the Triple Hook rule already in [voice-guide.md](../brand/voice-guide.md). The visual template itself (Hormozi-style big-text-block aesetic) is explicitly the wrong direction — it's also the most hustle-bro-coded visual signature available and would actively fight the Da Vinci/Aurelius identity target. Mechanic yes, template no.
- **Status:** not yet logged as a testable hypothesis — see experiments.md.
