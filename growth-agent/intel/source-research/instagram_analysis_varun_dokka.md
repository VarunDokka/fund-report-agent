## 5. What's Working

- **Exam-stress / student-burnout POV reels are your single strongest content pillar by a wide margin.** Your top 3 posts of the quarter are all in this lane, and they outperform everything else by 5–25x.
- **Internship/corporate-intern humor is your clear #2 pillar.** Not as explosive individually, but it shows up consistently in the 90–300 engaged range across 8+ posts — this is a real, repeatable niche, not a fluke.
- **Format:** low-production, direct-to-camera talking head with on-screen POV text. You don't need better production — the hook/caption is doing all the work.
- **Non-follower reach is excellent.** 97.7% of your reach this quarter came from people who don't follow you — the algorithm is actively pushing your best content into new eyeballs. Discovery is not your bottleneck.

## 6. What's Not Working

- **Niche/personal topics consistently underperform**: music taste (the "3 albums" post is an exception, likely due to trending audio), gym check-ins, football/social lifestyle tags, dating jokes, nostalgia bait, wellness walks. These cluster at the bottom of the list (85–150 engaged) even though some cost more effort (dating tattoo bit, gym filming).
- **Shares are at zero.** Not low — literally 0 across every post I checked, including your best one. Nothing in your content currently gives people a reason to send it to a specific friend.
- **Comments are near-zero relative to reach.** 10 comments on a 209K-view video is a comment rate of 0.005%. Nothing in your captions is prompting a reply or inviting disagreement/relatability confirmation.
- **The follower-conversion problem is the biggest issue on the account.** You had 6,260 profile visits and 373,545 accounts reached in 90 days, but you're sitting at 170 total followers. Even a mediocre 2–3% profile-visit-to-follow rate would put you at 300+ new followers just from this quarter's traffic alone — something in your bio, grid, or first-3-posts is failing to convert curious visitors into follows.

## 7. Recommendations

1. **Make "exam-stress/student-life POV" your core content pillar, not just your best-performing accident.** You're already good at it — 3 of your last ~10 weeks of posting produced 65% of all views. Post more of these, faster, especially around exam periods, deadlines, and results days — the format has clear seasonal peaks (May was exam season, hence the concentration).

2. **Build "internship/corporate intern" into a named series.** You have 8+ posts already living in this lane at consistent 90–300 engagement. Give it a recognizable format (same caption style: "POV: you're an intern and...") so people start expecting and seeking it out — this is what turns a topic into a "series" people follow you for.

3. **Cut the niche/personal content or make it rarer.** Gym check-ins, dating bits, football, nostalgia, and wellness posts are your weakest performers and don't match what's clearly resonating (students/interns/uni life). If you want to keep posting them for yourself, fine — but don't expect them to grow the account, and don't let them dilute a page that's starting to have a clear identity.

4. **Fix the follower-conversion leak.** With this much reach, your bio and pinned/first-grid content need to explicitly answer "why follow." Add a clear one-line value prop to your bio (e.g. "uni & intern life, told straight" — something more specific than the current stat list), and consider a pinned post that's your best exam-stress video, since that's what's actually pulling people in.

5. **Add a reason to comment.** End captions with a direct, low-effort prompt tied to the joke ("comment your worst bed-rotting excuse" / "drop your uni below"). Right now nothing invites a reply, and comments are a major algorithmic signal you're leaving on the table.

6. **Add a reason to share.** Zero shares across the board means no one currently sees your content as "this is so [name], I have to send it." Try direct-address hooks like "send this to the friend who's currently floating into the stars" — tagging/sending prompts are one of the highest-leverage, lowest-effort changes you can make.

7. **Keep it 100% Reels** — that's already where 99%+ of your interactions come from, and static posts/carousels aren't pulling weight. No need to diversify format, just tighten the topic focus within Reels.
