## 0. Decode the account you're actually copying first: @nicktarmo

Before the 10, this matters more — you have his full scripts. Here's his formula, reverse-engineered from 20 transcripts:

**Hook (first 1-2 sentences, zero lead-in):**
- Scale/FOMO framing: *"The biggest gold rush in the past 25 years is right now, but you're getting left behind."*
- Framework-promise: *"This is my map on how to get out of a slump."* / *"This is my graph of human potential."*
- Listicle: *"10 out of 10 ways to build disgustingly strong viral sense."*
- Curiosity flip: *"This might surprise you."* / *"This may sound crazy to you."*

**The signature clarity-promise line** (appears in almost every video, right after the hook): *"Once I explain this to you, it's going to become disgustingly clear [why/what/where]..."* — this is doing huge work. It promises the viewer a specific payoff (clarity) instead of vague value, which is why people sit through 60-90 seconds of dense talking.

**Status/anti-neediness pattern interrupt:** *"You can watch this video or don't. I don't really care."* Shows up constantly. It's a confidence flex that also functions as a pattern interrupt mid-hook — breaks the "influencer asking for your attention" energy, which paradoxically holds attention.

**Structure:** problem → naming the invisible mechanism (DMN, dopamine hole, code/media leverage) → numbered or sequential protocol → payoff/proof (his own numbers: 19M views, 45K followers/month, $100K in a year). Nearly every video is teach-a-framework, not just an opinion.

**Tonality:** confident bordering on contrarian, second-person direct address ("you"), zero filler words, data-backed (always cites a real number), slightly clinical/psychological vocabulary (DMN, dopamine, agency, orchestrator).

**Pacing:** written and scripted first (he says this explicitly in video 7 — teleprompter via Instagram Edit app, then AI-assisted editing to cut dead air), which is why delivery is so dense. No breaths, no "um," no re-explaining.

**CTA:** "comment 'guide'/'prompts' and I'll send it over" — turns passive viewers into commenters, which the algorithm rewards, without asking for a follow.

This is your baseline template. Everything below is about layering *other* formats on top of it so your account doesn't feel one-note.

## 1. Hook-First Talking Head / Viral Value (your core format — closest to Nick's own)

## 6. How to actually dissect these (do this weekly, not once)

Nick's own process, from his transcripts, is worth copying exactly:
1. Pick one high-performing format per creator above.
2. Find their outlier video — 3-5x their normal view count relative to follower count.
3. Watch it with sound off first — note only what's on screen and when (text timing, cuts, framing changes). This isolates the *visual* pacing from the *verbal* hook.
4. Watch again with sound, transcribe the first 3 seconds word-for-word. That's your hook library.
5. Note the caption's opening line separately — captions and on-screen hooks often do different jobs (caption = searchable/context, on-screen = attention).
6. Log it somewhere (a note, a doc) under the matching bucket above, so after a month you have a swipe file of 10-15 real hooks per format instead of vague vibes.

This is literally what Nick describes doing with his AI tool (Stanley) in transcripts 3, 4, 7, 11, and 13 — the only difference is you're doing step 3-4 by eye instead of paying for a tool to do it.
