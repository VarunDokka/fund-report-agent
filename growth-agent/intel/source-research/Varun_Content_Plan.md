# The Actual Goal (say this on Day 1, out loud, to camera)

Not "documenting the climb." That's too soft to carry 138 videos. The real goal, stated plainly:

**Freedom — money, body, and time that nobody else controls.** Not rich for the sake of a number. Free: not checking the price before you buy something, not needing anyone's permission, strong enough and disciplined enough that nothing about your life is out of your hands.

Every series in this doc is in service of that one thing. Vegetarian fitness = the body part. PE/VC = the money part. Prayer/discipline/bymiilan lines = the mind part. This is what makes 138 videos feel like one story instead of scattered content — the viewer isn't following "a finance and gym guy," they're following someone chasing the exact thing they also want, and they're watching to see if you get there.

**Day 1 script (talking head, at home, direct to camera — this is the one video where your face and eye contact matter most):**

> "I'm turning 20 in two days. I don't want a job I'm stuck in, a body I'm not proud of, or to spend my life checking the price before I buy something. I want to actually be free — money, body, mind, none of it running my life instead of the other way round. So from today I'm putting the whole process on here. The gym, the internship, trying to break into venture capital, praying every morning, the mistakes, all of it. This is entry one."

# Format Key — What Each Series Should Actually Look Like

- **Richie Arc 1 & 2 ("day X" entries):** voice memo over B-roll, never straight to camera. Reflective and quiet, and it sidesteps your camera-awkward problem entirely.
- **bymiilan quote/emotion entries:** voiceover or on-screen text over slow, still footage (praying, lifting, reading). No talking head — the power is the stillness.
- **Veg/Fitness opinion posts ("I used to...", "stop blaming...", "why isn't it working"):** talking head, at home, direct address. Hot takes hold watch time when delivered with eye contact.
- **"What I eat" / protein-food posts:** slideshow of photos, text overlay, short voiceover. Proof content — people save these for the food, not your face.
- **POV entries:** mini skit, not explanation — act out both sides, or use on-screen text as the other person's line. This is what gets sent between friends.
- **PE/Finance explainer or reflection posts:** talking head, desk/laptop B-roll cut in where useful.
- **London Intern Diaries / day-in-the-life:** voiceover over a B-roll montage — commute, desk, gym, dinner. Classic low-edit vlog format.
- **A Few Years Ago vs Now + Building More Than Muscle:** before/after split — old photo or clip next to current footage, voiceover over the top. One of the strongest current Instagram formats because it's a visual pattern-interrupt before anyone's even read the caption.
- **Personal Growth confessions:** talking head, home, no B-roll needed. Vulnerability lands hardest with direct eye contact.
- **Supplements/junk food callout:** single photo, text-on-screen, short voiceover. It's a one-liner, don't over-produce it.

# Part 4: bymiilan-Style Series — Discipline, Mastery, Reading, Quotes (Emotion-Led)

Same cinematic, quiet, aphoristic format — a line of wisdom over calm B-roll (gym, morning light, a book, a walk). The change: every entry now opens by naming the exact emotional state the viewer is probably in right now, so they feel caught out / seen before the line even lands. That's the mechanism — they see themselves in the hook, then you hand them the meaning. Delivered slow, on-screen text or voiceover, no CTA.

Format: **Emotion → Hook (the "if you're feeling this" opener) → Line (the payoff)**

**Anger / frustration at yourself**
Hook: "if you're angry at yourself right now because you said you'd start Monday and it's already Thursday..."
Line: "discipline was never about not breaking the promise. it's making it again anyway, quietly, with no audience."

**Envy / comparison**
Hook: "if you saw someone your age post a win today and felt something twist in your chest you didn't want to admit..."
Line: "that feeling isn't jealousy. it's your own ambition, pointed at the wrong person."

**Shame / self-disappointment**
Hook: "if you're embarrassed by how many times you've already restarted this..."
Line: "restarting was never the failure. staying stopped is the only one that counts."

**Exhaustion**
Hook: "if you're running on four hours of sleep and still have to show up today..."
Line: "discipline was never built for the days you feel like it. that's what motivation was for, and motivation left weeks ago."

**Quiet hope**
Hook: "if there's a version of your life in your head that nobody else has seen yet..."
Line: "that's not delusion. that's the only proof you need before anyone else believes it."

**Impatience**
Hook: "if you're doing everything right and still can't see it, and it's making you want to stop..."
Line: "nothing that ever mattered showed up on the week you started needing it to."

**Loneliness in the grind**
Hook: "if it feels like nobody around you actually gets why you're doing this..."
Line: "you don't need them to get it. you just need to still be doing it when they eventually ask how."

**Fear of being behind**
Hook: "if everyone around you looks further ahead and it's making your chest tight..."
Line: "you're not behind. you're early in a story you just haven't finished telling yet."

**Quiet pride / unnoticed wins**
Hook: "if you did the small, boring thing today that nobody noticed..."
Line: "that's the whole game. nobody claps for the rep that actually builds you."

**Overwhelm / wanting too much at once**
Hook: "if you've got so much you want to do that you end up doing none of it..."
Line: "pick the one thing that scares you most and start there. everything else will still be waiting."

**Doubt about the path**
Hook: "if you're not even sure this is what you want anymore, just what you started..."
Line: "you're allowed to change direction. you're not allowed to use that as a reason to stop moving."

**Anger at being judged**
Hook: "if someone close to you made you feel small for actually trying..."
Line: "let them watch. that's the only response that's ever actually worked."

**Anxiety about time running out**
Hook: "if you lie awake doing the maths on how much time you've got left to figure this out..."
Line: "you have more time than you think, and less patience than you need. fix the second one first."

**Guilt over choosing comfort**
Hook: "if you keep picking comfortable over correct, and you already know it..."
Line: "comfort is just discipline's oldest excuse. it's rarely ever true."

**Genuine happiness / gratitude**
Hook: "if today actually went right and you almost forgot to notice..."
Line: "notice it anyway. that's the entire point of doing any of this."

**Restlessness / feeling stuck**
Hook: "if you feel like you're working hard and still standing still..."
Line: "some seasons aren't about moving forward. they're about getting strong enough to hold the next step when it finally comes."

Post 2-3x/week, standalone, no CTA, slow pacing over simple footage — you praying, lifting, reading, walking, or just sitting still. The emotion in the hook is the whole hook; the line is just the exhale after it.
