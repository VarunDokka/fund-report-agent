# ICP — Dream Follower

This file drifts slowly on purpose. `identity-curator` revisits it monthly against real commenter/engagement data and any explicit goal changes Varun states — it should not get rewritten by one good or bad week.

## Who's actually engaging right now (from data, last 90 days)

The top-performing content — exam-stress POV, internship/corporate-intern humor — is being watched, shared, and (rarely) commented on by people living the same specific moment: university students mid-exam-season and early-career/internship-stage young professionals, disproportionately in the UK given the specific references (British housing heat complaints, "LSE, Cambridge, Notts" status comparisons, "PE intern" acronym confusion). This is not a broad "Gen Z" audience — it's a narrow, high-recognition one, and the narrowness is exactly why the exam-stress posts hit 5–25x the account average. Nick Tarmo's own framing applies directly here: hyper-specific content that makes one narrow group feel personally read outperforms broad relatable content every time (see [guru-tracker.md](../intel/guru-tracker.md)).

## Working ICP description

**Primary:** a university student or early-career intern (18–23), UK-based or UK-adjacent, currently mid-grind — exams, applications, or a competitive internship — who feels behind their peers and is quietly ambitious about getting out of that feeling, not just complaining about it. They don't follow "motivational" accounts; they follow people who are visibly in the same fight and posting through it in real time.

**Secondary (aspirational layer, not yet data-validated):** someone slightly further along who wants the "freedom" narrative — financial independence, physical discipline, a documented interior life — and is drawn to the Da Vinci/Marcus Aurelius register described in [identity.md](identity.md): mastery across domains, restrained/reflective tone, not hype. This audience hasn't shown up in the data yet because the account hasn't posted enough of that material to be found by them. `growth-analyst` should start tracking this segment separately once the mastery/philosophy pillar has enough posts to measure (see [experiments.md](../coaching/experiments.md)).

## What this ICP explicitly is not

Not the hustle-culture/bro-preneur audience that follows accounts like Nick Tarmo's for the "how I made 6 figures" system-and-tools content. Those creators are a mechanics source for `trend-scout`, not a target audience — see the persona guardrail in [voice-guide.md](voice-guide.md). If engagement starts skewing toward that audience (comments asking "what's your business," "teach me your system"), that's a signal the tone has drifted off Da Vinci/Aurelius and toward guru-bait, and `daily-coach` should flag it.

## How this file gets updated

Monthly, by `identity-curator`, using: who's actually commenting (not just view counts), what they say, and any explicit goal changes logged in [goals.md](goals.md). Do not rewrite this off one viral post.
