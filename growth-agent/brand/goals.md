## Reality check (stoic coach reads the numbers, not vibes)

State the math plainly, once, so it's on record — then move on to what's actually controllable.

- Last 90 days produced 373,545 accounts reached, 6,260 profile visits, and a net gain that left the account at 170 total followers.
- Going from 170 → 10,000 needs roughly 9,830 net-new followers in 30 days — about a third of the timeframe that produced the account's entire current follower base, at ~33x the pace.
- Even at an excellent 3–5% profile-visit-to-follow rate (current quarter's actual rate is far below that — see [instagram_analysis_varun_dokka.md](../intel/source-research/instagram_analysis_varun_dokka.md)), hitting 9,830 new followers needs roughly 200,000–330,000 *profile visits* inside 30 days. The last 90 days produced 6,260 total. That's a 30–50x jump in visit volume, compressed into a third of the time — which itself requires several more 150K–200K+ view outliers than the account has produced in any prior month.
- This is not a reason to abandon the number. It's the reason the fix has to be structural, not incremental: the follower-conversion leak (guardrail #1 in [identity.md](identity.md)) has to close hard and fast, and the exam-stress/internship pillars need to fire at a rate the account hasn't hit yet. A 5–10x conversion-rate improvement plus sustained 1–3x/day posting in the two proven pillars is the only combination with a real shot at this number. Anything less and the account lands somewhere well short of 10,000, still ahead of where it started.
