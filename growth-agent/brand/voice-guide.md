# Voice Guide

Canon, not suggestions. Pulled from [production-playbook.md](../intel/source-research/production-playbook.md) and [creator-format-breakdown.md](../intel/source-research/creator-format-breakdown.md). `script-coach` applies these exactly; `daily-coach` enforces them in review.

## On-screen text density — match it to content type

- **Rolling word-by-word / constant captions** — explainer/teaching/listicle content. Text IS the information delivery.
- **One static sentence/question pinned the whole video** — short 15–25 sec single-punchline videos only.
- **"Anchor + Punch" (sparse, high-impact)** — story/philosophy/narrative content, including the mastery/stoic material. One text card at the open (the scroll-stopping claim), then no text until each quotable line gets its own synced text card. Rest of the video is just the face — keeps it feeling like a story, not a slideshow.

Rule: text density should match content density. Dense teaching = dense text. Story = sparse text at the 2–3 highest-leverage moments only.

## The Triple Hook — first 1–3 seconds, every video, no exceptions

Three attention mechanisms firing at once so it works muted, with sound, or on a half-second glance:
1. **Text hook** — on-screen claim, different wording than the spoken line.
2. **Verbal hook** — the spoken opener.
3. **Visual hook** — a pattern interrupt in frame (punch-in crop, unusual first image, movement). Visual *change* in the first 1–2 seconds is what actually stops a scrolling thumb.

## Tonality/pacing default

Restrained confidence — not hyped, not monotonous.
- Flat, matter-of-fact, unhurried. Closer to "I don't need your approval" than motivational-speaker enthusiasm.
- 2 deliberate pace breaks per video on the killer/quotable lines: slow down, drop the voice slightly, leave a real silent beat before continuing.
- No smiling/performed enthusiasm on philosophy/story content — flatness reads as credibility, not a pitch.

## Filming setup

Phone front camera is sufficient.
1. Face the window (key light in front, never behind). Diffuse harsh sun with a sheer curtain.
2. Stand 3–6 ft back — too close blows out highlights.
3. Tripod/stand required for sit-down/story content — no handheld.
4. Chest-up or shoulders-up, eyes in upper third.
5. Script as close to the lens as possible — off-axis reading breaks direct-to-camera intimacy.
6. Film near soft surfaces (rug/curtains/couch) — bare rooms echo, and audio quality affects retention more than video polish.
7. Background: personal but uncluttered — a shelf, plant, framed art. Not blank, not busy.
8. Two-take structure for punch-in opens: full wide take, then a closer re-film of the first 2 lines, cut close-up in for ~3 sec then to wide.

## CTA rule

**Never end on "Follow."** It's a low-conversion, generic ask. End on a comment-bait CTA tied to a specific value exchange, or a direct-address share prompt ("send this to the friend who's currently floating into the stars"). Comments and shares are algorithmically weighted harder than follows and directly address the account's two biggest data gaps (zero shares, near-zero comments — see [identity.md](identity.md)).

## Script content rule

Titles/hooks without a real personal story/proof behind them are just vibes — they read as poster quotes, not teaching. Before scripting any idea, confirm there's a specific, provable personal story attached. If there isn't, find one or deprioritize the idea.

## Persona guardrail (applies to every subagent, not just script-coach)

Nick Tarmo and similar hustle-culture creator-coaches are a **mechanics source only** — hook structure, posting cadence, algorithm behavior. Never adopt their persona, values, or bravado. The account's actual identity target is Da Vinci/Marcus Aurelius, per [identity.md](identity.md)'s Aspirational Archetype section. If a script starts reading like "here's how I made 6 figures," that's a persona drift, not a mechanic — cut it.
